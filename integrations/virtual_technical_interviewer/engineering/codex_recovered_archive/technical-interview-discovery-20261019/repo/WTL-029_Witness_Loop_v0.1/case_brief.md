## 1. The one-sentence mystery

A railway worker appears to have vanished aboard the last train, but his departure was staged to divert pursuit from an escape through the maintenance corridor; a dispatcher conceals the real timeline to protect her brother, whose deception threatens to frame him for the very disappearance he helped orchestrate.

## 5. Core mystery solution — three causal layers

1. **Factual question:** Did Zhou board the final train? **No**; the departure trail was intentionally staged.
2. **Causal question:** Why stage a train departure? To divert pursuit while Zhou left via a staff route to protect documentary evidence of potential safety-record falsification.
3. **Moral question:** Is Lin Yu the missing person's kidnapper? The expanded canon says **no**; he helped create misleading evidence, which may still have serious consequences. This is not a blanket moral or legal acquittal.

**Not established by V1:** a murderer, kidnapper, a criminal conviction, where Zhou hid, or the motive behind the deeper safety scandal.

## 8. Proposed v0.2 gameplay change contract (requires implementation)

**Scene 0, Observe:** player-first environmental exploration without Lin Wu's avatar; record observations only on inspection.

**Scene 1, Interview:** Lin Wu appears in same station composition, after off-screen phone narration; sequential narrator → witness → player → model-assisted witness flow; history accessible.

**Scene 2, Reconstruct:** permit deduction with inspected evidence and provenance; add a credible timestamp corroborator so the purported contradiction is meaningful.

**Scene 3, Verdict:** preserve a deterministic outcome evaluator. A more mature scoring model may require: inspected evidence + verified contradiction + reasoned causal explanation + optional relationship protection.

**No spoilers by leakage:** director canon is compiled into a trusted game authority and exposed via a state-filtered `released_facts` projector. It must not simply be sent as a long LLM system message at the first turn.

**Reward/goal split:** `discovery` (objects genuinely found), `deduction` (supported relationships), `resolution` (eligible verdict), optional `relationship` (meaningful remembered interaction). Lin Wu's probabilistic dialogue cannot grant any of these alone.

**Failure states:** distinguish submitted false accusation, submitted partial resolution, completed truth, unfinished but recoverable, and exhausted/no valid next route. A deadlock needs an explicit recovery/hint or distinct failure treatment; present v0.1 does not implement it.
