## 3. Narrated opening and playable scene state machine

Build a genuine front-end/game opening progression (not a full rewrite of the current three verdict scenes):

1. **Title / case assignment:** a short non-spoiler case briefing. Who: Zhou Xu reported missing; where: fictional Yunjiang Railway Station; player: investigator; goal: determine whether he boarded and reconstruct the evidence. Do not claim that Lin Wu definitely reported him unless it is approved by the author.
2. **Narrator voice / pre-arrival audio cue:** on entering, player hears the last trace of a nervous woman's call; she notices approaching footsteps and cuts it short. The player does **not** yet know caller or time. Make it distinct from the earlier 00:17 call.
3. **EMPTY rainy platform observation:** no NPC portrait in frame. The player can survey a dark, rain-soaked railway platform with a fading last-train tail light, a subtle clock high on a station beam, red fabric/raincoat near lockers, and a folded train ticket on a bench. Permit zoom/inspect on distinct hotspots.
4. **Approach witness:** player explicitly chooses to meet Lin Wu. They are not forced to inspect every item; ignored clues remain missed, not magically recorded. Make missing clues recoverable later where necessary to avoid accidental deadlock.
5. **Lin Wu entrance:** smoothly transition to the **same exact station composition** with Lin Wu entering, visibly anxious, sleep-deprived, hurried and guilty-but-controlled; character must not cover the clock, coat or bench. First nervous greeting appears as her caption, with input disabled until she finishes and user taps/clicks Continue.
6. **Sequential dialogue:** alternating Lin Wu response/subtitle → continue/acknowledge → player input → pending/thinking state → validated NPC reply; read-only conversation history and evidence notebook remain accessible throughout.
7. **Evidence reasoning and verdict:** preserve evidence linking, scene progression and truth/partial/false-accusation endings, now tied to genuinely inspected evidence or a clearly versioned migration rule. Keep 6 case actions separate from free chatting and from billable API request count.

Use a separate onboarding/experience phase or an explicitly versioned game schema; **do not** introduce `scene=0` into `witness-state/v1` without migrating validators and saves. Preserve resumability for users who are already in the three-scene game. No destructive silent reset.

## 5. Evidence → questioning → deduction contracts

Define inspectables in a structured manifest/registry (id, scene, bounds, visual source, public description, observation condition, evidence type, knowledge provenance, disclosure effect, tests):

| ID | Visible clue | Click/zoom reveals | It does NOT prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clock` | Subtle station clock | stopped at **00:17** | that the train left at 00:17 or Lin Wu lied |
| `red_fabric` | red fabric near lockers | red raincoat/fibers | whose it is or whether Zhou wore it |
| `ticket` | folded item on bench | unused-looking ticket, **周序 / Zhou Xu** | that he certainly never boarded |
| `tail_light` | distant red departure lights | a train-like trace departing | precise time or missing passenger's presence |
| `overheard_call` | offscreen sound/caption | woman cuts a call short on seeing investigator | caller identity or connection to 00:17 |

`seen != inspected != authenticated != interpreted != linked`. Do not auto-unlock evidence simply because artwork was shown. The player can phrase questions naturally in Chinese or English; map them to intent/topics without relying solely on brittle exact keywords. Recommended topics: `timeline`, `call_recent`, `call_0017`, `raincoat`, `ticket`, `relation`, `promise`, `whereabouts`, `free_chat`. Keep 'recent call' vs '00:17 brother's call' disjoint. Case questions spend actions according to validated engine rules; ordinary conversation and notebook inspection do not. UI must show what consumes an action before confirmation.

Important migration: v0.1 starts with `clock` already acquired and grants `ticket` on Scene 2; new observe-first gameplay must not pretend that v0.1 behavior already required inspection. Implement a clear v0.2 state contract or backward-compatible observation layer with documented handling of old saves, tested with both fresh and existing session records. Preserve at least one reachable truth, partial, and false-accusation path, and do not make all paths impossible when the player missed a nonmandatory hotspot.

## 6. Conversation interface UX

Design the UI as scene-dominant, while preserving three complementary utilities: **evidence notebook**, **conversation timeline**, **NPC relationship/context**. Scene is the hero view. Conversation is sequential not a permanent wall of message bubbles.

- Narrator captions have a different voice/skin from Lin Wu dialogue; player statements are separately attributed.
- When Lin Wu speaks, show a portrait-linked subtitle close to/under her without blocking evidence; click/tap/Continue to dismiss and reveal the next player turn.
- The player has one clear input field when it is their turn; after Send display pending state, prevent duplicate sends, and handle timeout/provider failure without accidental state commit.
- History drawer / left navigation preserves all actual exchanges with speaker, scene, time/order and expandable earlier replies. Opening this history does not use case actions or API requests.
- Evidence drawer shows discovered, examined, testimonial, validated and hypothesized information separately. Tooltips/case labels must not spoil unknown facts.
- Support Mandarin and English responses according to player preference; mixed input should not force an unrelated language switch.
- On the character layer, show anxiety/hesitation and progressively shift demeanor based on *validated* trust/fear, not random character resets. Free chat permitted but doesn't secretly advance case milestones.
- Preserve clear API activation state, the local 20-request cap (unless separately approved to change it), no-key simulated mode, native key isolation and non-destructive reconnect.
- Never use HTTP or remote frontend assets for the station artwork; images ship locally inside the Mac bundle.
